Here is a simplified, easy-to-understand breakdown of the **SHS/TVET School Selection Guidelines**:

---

## 📋 General Selection Rules

* **Total Schools:** You must select **8 schools** in total. Rank them in order of priority (Choice 1 is your overall favorite).
* **Boarding vs. Day:** You must pick **5 Boarding schools** and **3 Day schools**.
* **Programs:** Pick your preferred course of study and accommodation choice for each school.
* **No Duplicates:** **Never repeat a school** on your list, even under a different status or program.
* **Day School Location:** All 3 Day schools must be within your local catchment area (schools near your home that you can easily commute to daily).
* **Final Placement:** Once you are placed in any of your 8 choices, **the decision is final and cannot be changed**.

---

## 🏫 Category Rules (How to Pick Your 8 Schools)

* **Category A:** Maximum of **2 schools**.
* **Category B:** Maximum of **3 schools** (e.g., up to 2 Boarding and 1 Day).
* **Category C / Pilot Private SHS:**
* You can select up to 2 Boarding and 1 Day for Category C.
* You can select Pilot Private SHS under Category B or C as a Day option.
* **Optionally:** You can choose to pick **all 8 schools** from Category C / Pilot Private SHS (5 Boarding and 3 Day).



---

## ⚙️ Special Academic Tracks

* **TVET Only Track:** If you want a purely technical or vocational pathway, select all **8 TVET Institutes** across Categories A, B, and C (Day or Boarding).
* **STEM / Science Track:** If you aim for careers in Engineering, Medicine, Space Science, etc., select **STEM/Science** for all **8 choices** across Categories A, B, and C (Day or Boarding).

---

## ✍️ Final Step

* The completed school selection form **must be signed by a parent or guardian** and submitted to your **JHS Headteacher**.
